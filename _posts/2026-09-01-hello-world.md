Welcome! I've rebuilt this site as a home for my work in Clinical NLP and the
apps I build on the side.

A quick tour of what you'll find on the [projects page](/#projects):

- **[Medmorf](https://medmorf.com)** — on-device AI for medical documents:
  translation, anonymization, summarization, and transcription, with nothing
  leaving the browser.
- **[TrialSchema](https://putssander.github.io/trialschema/)** — structuring
  clinical trial criteria so LLMs can match patients to trials.
- **[Metaphor Discovery](https://putssander.github.io/4dpicture-danish/demo/index.html)**
  — a research demo on finding the metaphors people use to describe illness,
  part of the 4D PICTURE project.
- **[Actuelly](https://actuelly.com)** — a personal news project for iPhone
  and Mac, coming soon.

I'll use this blog for notes on Clinical NLP research, app development, and
whatever sits in between.
